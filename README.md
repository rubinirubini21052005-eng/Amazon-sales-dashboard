# Amazon Sales Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Amazon sales data using SQL and Power BI to uncover business insights related to sales performance, customer behavior, product categories, discounts, and regional trends.

The project combines SQL-based data analysis with Power BI visualizations to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Objectives

* Analyze revenue performance across product categories.
* Track monthly sales trends.
* Identify top-performing regions.
* Evaluate the impact of discounts on revenue.
* Analyze customer ratings and reviews.
* Build an interactive dashboard for business reporting.

---

## Tools Used

* PostgreSQL
* Power BI
* Power Query
* DAX
* Microsoft Excel

---

## Dataset Features

The dataset contains:

* Product ID
* Product Category
* Order Date
* Customer Region
* Revenue
* Discount Percentage
* Rating
* Review Count
* Payment Method

---

## SQL Analysis Performed

### 1. Revenue by Product Category

Calculated total revenue generated by each product category.

### 2. Monthly Revenue Trend

Analyzed month-wise revenue performance.

### 3. Regional Revenue Ranking

Ranked customer regions based on revenue contribution.

### 4. Payment Method Analysis

Identified preferred payment methods across regions.

### 5. Average Rating Analysis

Compared customer satisfaction levels by region.

### 6. Discount Impact Analysis

Measured how discount levels affect revenue generation.

### 7. Product Performance Analysis

Identified products with high reviews but low ratings.

### 8. Revenue Classification

Classified categories as High Revenue and Low Revenue.

### 9. Revenue Contribution Percentage

Calculated each category's contribution to total revenue.

### 10. Top Categories by Region

Identified the top three performing categories in every region.
